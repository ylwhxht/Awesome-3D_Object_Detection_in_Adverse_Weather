Sensor Perspective

Adverse weather is not one problem. It is a sensor-specific distribution shift.

Robust 3D object detection emerges from understanding how each modality fails, then designing fusion policies that adapt as the environment changes.

LiDAR

High-resolution geometry, vulnerable to backscatter, attenuation, near-range clutter, and sparse long-range returns.

Camera

Dense semantics and texture, degraded by fog, glare, darkness, wet lenses, motion blur, and low contrast.

Radar

Weather-resilient ranging and Doppler cues, challenged by sparsity, noise, elevation uncertainty, and clutter.

V2X

Spatial diversity from infrastructure and nearby agents, constrained by calibration, latency, bandwidth, and trust.
